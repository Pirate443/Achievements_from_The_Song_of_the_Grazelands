--------------------------------------------------------------
			Description:
This is a small mod - integration, adds five achievements for the mod 'The Song of the Grazelands' based on 'The Achievement Framework'.
Can be connected before or after completing quests. If connected after completion, achievements will unlock upon loading a save.
--------------------------------------------------------------
			Requirements:
Morrowind
Tribunal
Bloodmoon
Morrowind Code Patch
MGE XE 0.17.0 + (Run MWSE-update.exe)
The Achievement Framework
The Song of the Grazelands
--------------------------------------------------------------
			Installation:
Copy the "Data Files" to your game folder or use a mod manager.
